03 — The SSF Method™

Four phases.
One direction.

The method has been proven on three continents and two companies. We have lost touch with the third.

  1. 01 Weeks 1–2

    Selection of the relic

    We identify the systems whose documentation is already lukewarm, then leave them strictly in place. The exercise is often experienced as a relief.

    Deliverable — Register of systems we will not touch again

  2. 02 Months 3–18

    Set-aside

    No more updates, migrations or tickets. We fix nothing: we let time apply its patch, which never misses.

    Deliverable — Non-intervention certificate

  3. 03 Months 18–36

    Departure of the knowers

    We organise natural rotation: retirement, a distant promotion, a transfer. No handover is planned, none is needed.

    Deliverable — Non-transmission certificate

  4. 04 Permanent

    Maintenance of ignorance

    Annual ritual: we check that nobody has learned anything. Any curious employee is immediately promoted into another department.

    Deliverable — Unlearning committee

Method certified by an ethics committee we have lost contact with.
